## Cross-area dependencies

iOS adoption consumes the validated bundle owned by the skill-suite roadmap. Its prerequisite is a usable installed suite; application selection, requirements, and simulator/device acceptance belong to the adopting feature plan. Completing bundle work does not complete the adoption journey. Evidence from adoption can justify later skill improvements, each owned by a plan in the skill-suite roadmap.
